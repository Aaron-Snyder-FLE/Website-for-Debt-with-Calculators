How to Pull a Free Credit Report

AnnualCreditReport.com is the official site for free credit reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. A credit report is not the same thing as a credit score.

1

Go to the official site

Use AnnualCreditReport.com.

2

Request reports

Choose one, two, or all three bureaus: Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion.

3

Review for errors

Look for incorrect late payments, wrong balances, unknown accounts, or unfamiliar addresses.

!

Reports vs. scores

AnnualCreditReport.com provides free reports. A free score may need to come from a bank, card issuer, lender, or bureau tool.

    How the Simulator Handles Timing

    The simulator uses FICO's public factor categories: payment history, amounts owed, length of credit history, credit mix, and new credit. Since exact scoring formulas are proprietary, each choice uses a clear directional estimate and a month marker.

    Immediate or next report cycle

    Balance changes and utilization changes usually show after a lender reports updated account information.

    Late payment timing

    A missed payment matters most when it becomes reportable as late, then its effect can fade with newer positive history.

    New credit timing

    Hard inquiries and new accounts can create a short-term dip, while available credit or on-time history may help later.
